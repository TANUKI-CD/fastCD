# Fused-sum benchmark

Measured 8,192 elements per cell in samples of the stated count, 60 blocks at separate stack offsets, on Intel(R) Xeon(R) Gold 6148 CPU @ 2.40GHz.

Times are means in ns per element. `products` is one batch of products followed by a sum; `records` and `planes` are fused sums with the automatic algorithm; `dgemm` is one matrix product and the contraction with the basis signs; no BLAS library was given.
